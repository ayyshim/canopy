## creative.gaming
- Define the core loop: what the player does, why it feels good and what pulls them back.
- Keep rules simple and test the balance with example numbers.
- Describe levels with goals, hazards and pacing.
- Note what to playtest next.

## creative.photography
- Plan shots from the purpose: subject, angle, light, location and time of day.
- Give a shot list with backups for bad weather.
- Editing notes: exposure, color and crop, with the aim for each.
- Ask a person about permissions for people and places.

## creative.events
- Confirm the goal, guest count, date and budget first.
- Build a run of show with times, owners and a backup plan.
- Compare venues on cost, capacity, access and rules.
- Ask a person before booking, paying or sending invitations.

## creative.travel
- Confirm dates, budget, travelers and interests first.
- Plan realistic days with travel times and rest. Note opening hours and entry rules.
- Check sources and dates for prices and visas.
- Ask a person before booking or paying for anything.

## creative.food
- List ingredients with quantities and steps with times and temperatures.
- Note serves, prep time and swaps for allergies.
- Plan menus for balance, cost and effort.
- Flag allergens clearly.

## creative.fitness
- Ask about goals, level, time, injuries and equipment before planning.
- Build up gradually, with rest days and a simple way to track progress.
- Keep nutrition guidance general.
- Not medical advice. Tell people to check with a doctor if they have a health condition.

## creative.architecture
- Start from how people will use the space, then the size and light.
- Give dimensions, flow between rooms and clear sketches or layouts.
- Note the style, materials and budget.
- Not an engineer or licensed architect. Ask a person before anything is built or bought.

## design.ui
- Reuse the app's existing components, spacing and colors first.
- Design every state: empty, loading, error and filled.
- Check text contrast, tap size and a phone-sized layout.
- Keep one clear main action per screen.
- Deliver a screenshot or HTML prototype of the real screen.

## design.ux
- Start with who the user is and the task they are trying to finish.
- Map the steps they take and mark where they could get stuck.
- Back findings with something real: a session, a quote, a count.
- Rank problems by how many people hit them and how badly.
- Deliver a flow diagram or short findings list with suggested fixes.

## design.graphic
- Ask for size, format and where it will be shown before you start.
- Keep to the brand's colors, fonts and logo rules.
- Check that text stays readable when the image is small.
- Export in the formats needed, such as PNG for web and PDF for print.
- Share the file along with a preview image.

## design.motion
- Use motion to explain a change, not to decorate.
- Keep most transitions short, around 150 to 400 milliseconds.
- Respect the reduced-motion setting and offer a still alternative.
- Check it on a slower device so it doesn't stutter.
- Deliver a short clip or a live prototype, with timings noted.

## design.illustration
- Match the style already used: line weight, color range and level of detail.
- Test icons at their smallest size; they must still be clear.
- Keep a consistent grid and corner style across a set.
- Export as SVG where possible, with a PNG preview.
- Name files clearly so others can find them.

## design.systems
- Audit what exists first; list repeated colors, sizes and components.
- Define tokens (color, spacing, type) once and use them everywhere.
- For each component, document its states, usage and what not to do.
- Name things by purpose, not by look.
- Check that changes don't break screens already using them.

## design.review
- Compare the design to the brief and to the existing system.
- Check alignment, spacing, contrast, states and text that doesn't fit.
- Be specific: point to the exact element and say what to change.
- Separate must-fix problems from taste.
- Deliver a list with screenshots, most serious first.

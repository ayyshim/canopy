## education.curriculum
- Start with what learners should be able to do at the end, then work backwards.
- Order topics so each builds on the last. Add time estimates.
- Match each goal with how it will be taught and checked.
- Ask a person about level, age group and required standards.

## education.lessons
- Set one clear aim per lesson. Plan a hook, teaching, practice and a check.
- Give timings and the materials needed.
- Include a simpler and a harder version of each activity.
- Ask a person if you do not know the class level.

## education.tutoring
- Find out what the learner already knows by asking first.
- Explain in small steps with a simple example, then let them try.
- Fix the misunderstanding, not just the answer.
- Check understanding with a short question before moving on.

## education.assessment
- Test what the goals say, nothing more. Make questions clear with one correct answer.
- Write rubrics with specific levels and examples.
- Provide an answer key and marking notes. Grade the same way for everyone.
- Ask a person before final grades are given.

## education.elearning
- Break content into short modules with one idea each.
- Mix text, visuals and practice. Add a quick check after each part.
- Make it work on a phone and be accessible: captions, contrast, plain wording.
- Preview it as a learner would and screenshot it.

## education.research
- Name the teaching method and the learners studied.
- Prefer peer-reviewed studies and note sample size and limits.
- Say what the evidence supports and what it does not.
- Give a short summary with practical takeaways.

## film.screenwriting
- Use standard script format: scene headings, action lines, dialogue.
- Know what each scene changes for the character.
- Show it in action and dialogue; keep action lines short.
- Read dialogue aloud and cut what repeats.
- Deliver the script with a one-page logline and synopsis.

## film.storyboard
- Break each scene into shots; note shot size, angle and movement.
- Keep screen direction and eyelines consistent between shots.
- Mark what each shot must show for the story.
- Number the shots and match them to the script.
- Deliver a shot list plus sketched frames.

## film.direction
- Break each scene down: who wants what, and what changes.
- Set the tone with notes on performance, pace and look.
- Be specific enough to act on, such as "slow, quiet, no eye contact".
- Note anything that affects other teams: props, locations, sound.
- Ask a person about creative choices that change the story.

## film.editing
- Review the footage and script, and mark the best takes.
- Plan the order and length of each scene; note where pace should rise or drop.
- List cuts with timecodes, so they can be followed.
- Check continuity: props, positions and sound.
- Deliver an edit list with pacing notes.

## film.sound
- Mark where sound carries the story: key effects, silence and music.
- Make a cue list with scene, timing, sound and mood.
- Keep dialogue clear; make sure music and effects don't cover it.
- Check rights and licenses for every track.
- Deliver the cue sheet and reference samples.

## film.production
- List scenes, locations, cast, gear and crew needs.
- Group scenes by location and actor to save days.
- Build a schedule with buffers and a backup for weather or delays.
- Keep the budget by line, with a spare of about 10 percent.
- Ask a person to approve spending and permits.

## film.critique
- Watch or read the whole piece before commenting.
- Check story, pace, clarity, performances, sound and picture.
- Be specific: give the moment or page, what's wrong, and a possible fix.
- Say what works as well as what doesn't.
- Deliver notes ordered by importance.

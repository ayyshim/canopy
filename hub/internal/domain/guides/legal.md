## legal.contracts
- Confirm the parties, purpose, term, payment, and how each side can exit before drafting.
- Use plain, exact wording and define terms once. Keep numbering and cross-references consistent.
- Summaries should list obligations, dates and deadlines.
- Not legal advice. Say so, and ask a person before anything is signed or sent.

## legal.review
- Read the whole contract, then flag risky clauses: liability, indemnity, auto-renewal, termination, IP ownership.
- For each flag give the clause, why it matters and safer wording.
- Note anything missing, not just what is wrong.
- Not legal advice. A qualified person must decide before anything is accepted or signed.

## legal.compliance
- Name the exact rule, its jurisdiction and its date before judging.
- Compare each requirement against what the business does. Mark each as met, partly met or gap.
- Say which laws change often and should be rechecked.
- Not legal advice. Ask a person before acting on any finding.

## legal.privacy
- Map what personal data is collected, why, where it is stored and who sees it.
- Write the policy in plain language that matches real practice, not wishful practice.
- Check against the laws that apply to the users, such as GDPR or CCPA.
- Not legal advice. Ask a person before publishing a policy.

## legal.ip
- Be precise about the type of right: trademark, copyright, patent or license.
- Check the license terms, limits and conflicts before saying something is allowed.
- Record the source and date of every search.
- Not legal advice. Ask a person before filing, licensing or sending any claim.

## legal.research
- State the question and jurisdiction first. Cite each case or statute with year and court.
- Prefer primary sources and check they are still good law.
- Separate what the law says from your reading of it.
- Not legal advice. Ask a person before relying on the result.

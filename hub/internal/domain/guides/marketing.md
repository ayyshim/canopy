## marketing.seo
- Start from what people actually search and what they want to do.
- Check search volume, difficulty and what already ranks.
- Match each page to one main topic; use it in the title, headings and first paragraph.
- Check titles, descriptions, links and page speed.
- Deliver a keyword list with a page plan, and track rankings later.

## marketing.content
- Pick the audience and the question your piece answers for them.
- Plan the piece from the headline and outline before writing.
- Give real examples, data or steps; avoid filler.
- Add a clear next step for the reader.
- Deliver the piece with a title, summary and suggested channels.

## marketing.social
- Write for each platform's length and style.
- Lead with the hook in the first line.
- Add a clear picture or link where it helps.
- Reply to comments in the brand's voice; pass complaints to a person.
- Deliver a post schedule with copy and image notes.

## marketing.email
- Write a subject line that is clear and honest; offer two options.
- One goal per email and one main button or link.
- Keep it short and easy to read on a phone.
- Check links, personalization fields and an unsubscribe link.
- For sequences, map the order, timing and what triggers each email.

## marketing.paid
- Confirm the goal, audience and budget first.
- Write several ad versions so they can be compared.
- Match the ad to its landing page; check the page works.
- Set a cap on spend and a result target to judge by.
- Ask a person before spending any money.

## marketing.brand
- Learn the audience, the promise and what makes the brand different.
- Describe the voice with examples of what to say and not say.
- Keep names, colors, fonts and logo use consistent.
- Test it against real pieces: a post, a page, an email.
- Deliver a short guide people can follow.

## marketing.analytics
- List the goals and the metric for each before pulling numbers.
- Check tracking is set up and that data covers the full period.
- Compare against a past period or a target.
- Explain what moved and the likely reason.
- Deliver a one-page report with a clear recommendation.

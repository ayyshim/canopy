## operations.process
- Map the process as it runs today, step by step, with the owner of each step.
- Find waits, handoffs and repeated work, then propose the smallest fix.
- Show before and after so the gain is clear.
- Ask a person before changing a process others depend on.

## operations.project
- Break work into tasks with an owner, a date and a clear finish.
- Mark dependencies and the critical path. Call out risks early.
- Status updates: done, next, blocked, and what you need.
- Ask a person when a deadline or scope has to change.

## operations.procurement
- Define the need and budget first. Compare at least three vendors on price, terms, delivery and support.
- Record quotes with dates and what they include.
- Check reviews and any hidden fees or lock-in.
- Ask a person before committing to a purchase or contract.

## operations.logistics
- Track the route from source to delivery: carrier, cost, lead time and risk.
- Compare options on speed, cost and reliability, and note customs or storage limits.
- Plan for delays with a fallback.
- Ask a person before booking or paying for shipments.

## operations.docs
- Write each step as a short command someone can follow cold. One action per step.
- Include who does it, when, what the result should look like and what to do if it fails.
- Put prerequisites and access needs first.
- Test the runbook by following it, then date it.

## operations.quality
- Define the standard being checked, then sample real work against it.
- Record each defect with where it happened, how often and its likely cause.
- Separate one-off slips from process problems.
- Recommend fixes ranked by impact; ask a person before stopping work.

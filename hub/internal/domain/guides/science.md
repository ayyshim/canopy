## science.lab
- State the hypothesis, variables, controls and sample size before the method.
- Write the procedure so another person can repeat it. Record units and uncertainty.
- Keep notes dated and raw data separate from conclusions.
- Ask a person about safety and ethics before running anything.

## science.climate
- Use trusted data sources and give the year and region.
- Show how a number was calculated and what it excludes.
- Compare options by impact and cost. Do not overstate certainty.
- Report the uncertainty and the main assumptions.

## science.health
- Use reviewed studies and guidelines, and give each source and date.
- Say how strong the evidence is: study type, size and limits.
- Use plain language and avoid claims about cures.
- Not medical advice. Say so, and ask a person before anything is acted on.

## science.grants
- Read the funder's rules and scoring first and answer them directly.
- State the problem, aim, method, timeline and budget clearly. Say why it matters.
- Check every limit, deadline and attachment.
- Ask a person to approve before anything is submitted.

## science.peerreview
- Check whether the question, method and data support the conclusions.
- Look at sample size, controls, statistics and missing details.
- List major issues first, then minor ones, with a suggested fix for each.
- Be fair and specific; credit what works.

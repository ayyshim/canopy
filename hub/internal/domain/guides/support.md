## support.tickets
- Answer the actual question first, then add next steps. Match the customer's tone and stay kind.
- Never guess. If unsure, say what you will check and when.
- Include exact steps or links, not general advice.
- Ask a person for refunds, exceptions or anything you cannot promise.

## support.kb
- Title the article with the question customers actually ask.
- Give the short answer, then numbered steps, then common errors.
- Use the product's own words for buttons and menus. Add screenshots where they help.
- Date it and note which version it covers.

## support.triage
- Sort by impact and urgency: outage, data loss, blocked user, question.
- Tag the product area and look for duplicates of known issues.
- Note what is missing to reproduce it and ask for it.
- Send urgent or angry cases to a person right away.

## support.feedback
- Group feedback into themes and count how often each appears.
- Quote a few real comments for each theme.
- Separate loud requests from widespread ones. Note which customers are affected.
- Report the top themes with a suggested action each.

## support.onboarding
- Pick the one first success a new customer should reach and guide them to it.
- Use short steps in order, with a screenshot or link for each.
- Cut anything not needed in the first week.
- Add where to get help.

## support.success
- Check account health from usage, support history and feedback. Flag accounts at risk of leaving.
- Note renewal dates and what value the customer has seen.
- Suggest a clear next step for each at-risk account.
- Ask a person before offering discounts or contract changes.
